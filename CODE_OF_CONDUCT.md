# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
doxxing, and deliberate disclosure of another person's credentials or private
agent data are not acceptable.

Assume good faith while reviewing ideas, but require evidence for security and
compatibility claims. Disagree with designs rather than people. Maintainers may
edit, hide, or remove harmful content and restrict participation when needed.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers. Reports will be
handled with discretion and without retaliation.
